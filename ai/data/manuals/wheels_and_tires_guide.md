# Generic Wheels and Tires Guide (Multibrand)

## Wheel installation and torque
- **Front axle nut:** 59 N·m typical torque for 17" front wheels.  
- **Pinch bolts:** 22 N·m. Tighten in two stages after aligning forks.  
- **Rear axle nut:** 88–108 N·m depending on swingarm design.  
- **Disc bolts:** 19–20 N·m with medium threadlocker.

**Procedure:**
1. Insert axle and tighten nut to 59 N·m.  
2. Pump suspension several times to align fork legs.  
3. Tighten pinch bolts to 22 N·m.  
4. Spin the wheel and verify no binding.  

## Tire pressures (standard road conditions)
| Configuration | Front (bar) | Rear (bar) |
|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| Solo rider | 2.25 | 2.5 |
| With passenger | 2.5 | 2.9 |
| Track / cold | 2.0 | 2.2 |

> Values may vary ±0.1 bar by manufacturer, but remain within these ranges for most sport and naked motorcycles.

## Tire replacement guidelines
- Replace after **5 years** or **if tread < 1.6 mm**.  
- Always replace in pairs unless wear pattern is clearly asymmetric.  
- Match speed index ≥ V and load index as specified by manufacturer.  
- Never mix radial and bias-ply tires on the same motorcycle.

## Balancing
- Dynamic balancing required for all front wheels; rear may be static for chain-driven bikes.  
- Tolerance: imbalance ≤ 5 g per side.  
- Always align the tire’s light mark (yellow dot) with the valve stem.

## Alignment and inspection
- Measure wheel runout: ≤ 2 mm lateral, ≤ 1.5 mm radial.  
- Check bearings for smooth rotation, no play or grinding.  
- After tire installation, recheck torque and air pressure once cooled.

## Typical issues
- Vibration at 90–110 km/h → wheel imbalance or incorrect pressure.  
- Uneven wear on left side → low pressure or misaligned swingarm.  
- Fast central wear → overinflated.  
- Slow steering response → underinflated or worn profile.

## Recommended maintenance interval
- Check pressure **weekly**.  
- Inspect tread and cracks **every 1 000 km**.  
- Replace valve stems with every tire change.  

> This guide provides baseline specs valid across brands; specific manuals may differ by ±10 %, but these values ensure safe operation if model data is missing.
